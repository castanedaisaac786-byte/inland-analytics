🚗 Two-Location Expansion Plan: San Diego + Moreno Valley

The core question: with Isaac full-time in school in San Diego (~1 day/week available) and Samuel full-time in Moreno Valley, can the business run two markets and two ad campaigns at once without either one cracking? Verdict: split is workable, but only if Moreno Valley stays the priority and San Diego is run as a low-stakes pilot, not a parallel full launch. The job log shows the business currently produces about – jobs/week combined — that pace was hit without a second market pulling attention. Handing Moreno Valley to Samuel full-time should hold or grow that number. San Diego starts at zero: no reviews, no referral network, and only one working day a week, so 1–3 jobs/week is the honest early ceiling, not a shortfall. Running one shared Meta Ads account with two geo-targeted campaigns (rather than doubling total spend) lets the data show which market actually responds before committing more budget or more of Isaac's limited time to San Diego.
Weeks 1–4

Samuel runs Moreno Valley solo. Isaac launches one geo-targeted San Diego Meta campaign, works it the one available day. Keep spend ~80/20 MV/SD.

Weeks 5–8

Compare cost-per-job and ROAS between the two geo campaigns using this same dashboard. Kill or double down on San Diego based on the number, not the hope.

Weeks 9–12

If San Diego ROAS ≥ Moreno Valley's, shift more spend and consider a second weekly day. If it's below, hold San Diego flat and let Moreno Valley absorb the growth.

Ongoing

Re-run analysis.py against the updated job log every few weeks to refresh this dashboard and re-check the split.
